# Fathom

A tiny cold light swims through a trench that has forgotten the sun. Follow the plankton, graze the flooded corridors, and uncover a path through the dark. Clear every mote and the next depth opens below you.

Your light grows as you feed. The maze opens inside a widening circle, revealing rock, branching passages, and the glimmers ahead. Stop eating and the light fades. The ground you have explored stays remembered, but beyond your circle the abyss swallows even familiar paths.

An amber bell could be a harmless drifter—or the Lanternjaw wearing its disguise. The eyeless Gloamfin follows sound, and every sonar pulse you send might help it find you. The Flarefish gives itself away in a sudden warm bloom, lighting straight through rock before closing in.

Read the wavefronts, turn tight corners, and leave ink between you and the hunters that see. Sonar can show the way around a bend; silence can buy you room to breathe. Each deeper maze holds more hunters, and your pulse reaches a little less far.
